About The Bag Spot

Our story, our mission and why we love what we do

Our Story

The Bag Spot started as a small passion project — a love for beautifully crafted bags and a belief that every woman deserves to carry something she truly loves. What began as a weekend market stall in Johannesburg has grown into a beloved neighbourhood boutique.

We source our bags from trusted local and international suppliers, selecting each piece for its quality, design and value. Our collection spans everyday essentials to special-occasion statement pieces.

We are proud to be a small, independent business that puts customers first — every single time.

Our Mission

To make boutique-quality bags accessible, affordable and easy to shop for every South African woman.

✦ Quality Selection

Every bag is hand-picked for craftsmanship, durability and style. We only stock what we would carry ourselves.

✦ Personal Service

We take the time to understand what you need. Whether in-store or online, you'll always feel looked after.

✦ Fair Pricing

Boutique quality doesn't have to mean boutique prices. We work hard to keep our collection accessible.

✦ Community First

We are a proudly South African business. We support local suppliers and give back to our community.

How This Website Helps

We replaced a manual, error-prone process with a simple digital solution — hosted on Amazon S3.

Before the Website

  • Orders via WhatsApp & phone calls
  • Bookings written in notebooks
  • Delayed confirmations
  • Mixed-up orders during busy periods
  • No organised customer records

After the Website

  • Online catalogue with product details & prices
  • Digital order form with unique reference number
  • Store visit booking with confirmation page
  • Instant order summary for customer & boutique
  • Hosted on Amazon S3 — always available, no server needed
