Why We Built This Website

We replaced a chaotic manual process with a simple, reliable digital experience.

The Old Way

  • Orders taken via WhatsApp & phone calls
  • Bookings tracked in notebooks
  • Delayed order confirmations
  • Mixed-up orders during busy periods
  • No central record of customer requests

The New Way

  • Online catalogue with clear product details
  • Digital order form with reference number
  • Store visit booking with confirmation
  • Instant order summary for customer & boutique
  • Hosted on Amazon S3 — always available
